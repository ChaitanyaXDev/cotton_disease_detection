How Each Algorithm Works

Understanding the core architectural differences and engineering trade-offs.

MobileNetV2
Transfer Learning
{{ (metrics_m2.get('test_accuracy', 0.8854) * 100)|round(2) }}% Test Accuracy
  • Starts with a model already trained on over 1 million everyday images (ImageNet), so it already knows edges, textures and shapes.
  • We keep that knowledge and only train a new final layer to recognize cotton diseases.
  • Uses lightweight depthwise separable convolutions, so it is fast and small.
  • Learns from less data and reaches higher accuracy.
  • Strength: best accuracy. Limitation: depends on a pretrained model.
Sequential 3-Block CNN
From Scratch
{{ (metrics_m1.get('test_accuracy', 0.7474) * 100)|round(2) }}% Test Accuracy
  • A CNN designed by us with 3 convolution blocks (32 → 64 → 128 filters).
  • Conv layers find patterns in leaf images, pooling shrinks the image, dropout reduces overfitting, and a final softmax layer gives the disease class.
  • Learns everything from our cotton dataset only, with no prior knowledge.
  • Strength: simple and fully customizable. Limitation: needs more data and training to match pretrained models.
