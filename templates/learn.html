Organic & Chemical Protocols

PlantPulse provides immediate disease diagnosis for cotton crops and provides actionable, dosage-calibrated solutions tailored to specific pathologies like Powdery Mildew and Bacterial Blight.

Standard Dosage Guidelines:
  • Mancozeb 75% WP: 50 gm per 15L spray pump
  • Carbendazim 50% WP: 50 gm per 15L spray pump
  • Neem Oil (Cold-Pressed Azadirachtin): 3-5 ml per liter of water
Recommendations follow scientific guidelines verified by agricultural research organizations.
